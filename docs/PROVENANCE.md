## Assets and publication boundaries

- The release excludes the upstream preview, soundtrack, mobile page, Wallpaper Engine package, remote font assets, and all reference-project media. The pinned HTML retains historical source references. The served page removes the font links and audio element and disables audio initialization. User-supplied runtime dependencies are listed separately. [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
- The repository and release should carry the original notice, identify this as a modified community project, and use the upstream noncommercial terms. Earlier MIT history does not supply the license for the pinned copy. No separate license for independent additions is proposed in v1.0.0. [LICENSE](../LICENSE) · [NOTICE](../NOTICE)
- Media remains a placeholder until reviewed. Use a capture made with the approved build, keep required attribution in visible captions or descriptions, and exclude monetization, sponsorship and promotional placements covered by the upstream terms. Direct reuse of upstream preview files or third-party media requires its own permission review.
- The audited noncommercial package presents no identified third-party license conflict. Commercial use, any change to the project's licensing boundary, and the rights to specific unpublished media are outside this determination. Resolve those questions before using the affected material or publishing under different terms.
